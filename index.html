Features

Everything you need to find your park crew

Park Pair is built from the ground up to make meeting new friends at amusement parks safe, easy, and fun.

📍

Park Check-In

Drop a pin at the park you're visiting and instantly see who's nearby. No guessing, no ghost profiles — just real people at the same place as you.

💬

In-App Chat

Message matched connections without ever sharing your phone number. Stay safe while you coordinate meetups and share excitement.

📸

Photo Verification

Prove you're really you with our automated face verification. Verified users get a badge so everyone knows they're genuine.

🎢

Ride Posts

Post what you're riding right now. Others at the park can see your plans and join you — spontaneity made simple.

🌙

Live Activities

Keep your ride status visible on your lock screen with iOS Live Activities. Your buddies always know where to find you.

🛡️

Report & Block

Feel unsafe? Report or block any user instantly. Our moderation team reviews every report to keep the community healthy.

How It Works

Three steps to your next adventure

From opening the app to riding together — it takes just minutes.

1

Check In

Open Park Pair when you arrive at the park. Your location is only shared while the app is open.

2

Connect

Browse nearby ride buddies, send a request, or start a chat. Find someone who loves the same thrills.

3

Ride Together

Meet up, hit the coasters, and make memories. All communication stays inside the app for your safety.

Safety

Built for peace of mind

Your safety is our top priority. Every feature is designed with protection in mind.

✅

Photo Verification

Automated face verification keeps profiles real and reduces catfishing.

🔒

No Phone Numbers

All chat happens inside the app. Your personal contact info stays private.

🛡️

Instant Blocking

Block anyone, anytime. Blocked users can't see or contact you again.

👤

Age-Gated Community

Park Pair is for ages 13+. We enforce age limits to keep the community appropriate.
