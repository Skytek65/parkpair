Child Safety Standards

Last updated: May 2025

Park Pair is committed to the safety and well-being of all users, especially minors. This page sets out our standards for child safety across the Park Pair app and all associated services operated by Park Pair.

1. Absolute Prohibition of CSAE

Park Pair has a strict zero-tolerance policy toward child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE). The following content and conduct are strictly prohibited on Park Pair:

  • Any content that sexually exploits or abuses a minor (child sexual abuse material — CSAM).
  • Grooming, solicitation, or any attempt to exploit a child sexually, emotionally, or financially.
  • Sharing, distributing, or requesting sexually explicit content involving minors.
  • Using Park Pair to facilitate in-person contact with a minor for the purpose of exploitation or abuse.
  • Any communication that sexualises a minor or attempts to initiate sexual conversation with a minor.

Violations of this policy will result in immediate and permanent account termination, and we will report confirmed cases to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and relevant law enforcement authorities as required by law.

2. Age Requirements

Park Pair requires users to be at least 13 years old to create an account. Users under 18 must have parental or guardian consent. We do not knowingly allow children under 13 to use the service. If we discover that a user is under 13, their account will be removed.

3. Safety Measures for Minors

Park Pair implements the following protections to keep minors safe:

  • Photo Verification: Users may be required to complete automated face verification to confirm their identity, reducing the risk of impersonation or deceptive profiles.
  • In-App Messaging Only: All communication is kept within the Park Pair app; personal contact details are never required to be shared.
  • Content Moderation: Profile content, photos, and messages are subject to automated and manual review.
  • Reporting Tools: Users can report any profile or message they believe violates these standards directly from within the app.
  • Block & Remove: Users can block and report other users at any time.

4. How to Report Child Safety Concerns

If you encounter content or behavior on Park Pair that you believe involves the sexual exploitation or abuse of a child, please report it immediately:

  • In-App Reporting: Tap the "Report" option on any profile or message within the Park Pair app.
  • Email: privacy@parkpair.app — use the subject line "Child Safety Report".
  • NCMEC CyberTipline: cybertipline.org
  • Local authorities: Contact your local law enforcement if a child is in immediate danger.

All child safety reports received by Park Pair are reviewed promptly. We preserve relevant data and cooperate fully with law enforcement investigations.

5. User Responsibilities

All Park Pair users agree, as a condition of using the app, to:

  • Not create, share, or distribute any content that exploits or sexualises minors.
  • Not use Park Pair to groom, solicit, or exploit any person under the age of 18.
  • Report any suspected child safety violations using the tools provided.

These obligations are incorporated into the Park Pair Terms of Service and are binding on all users.

6. Enforcement

Park Pair reserves the right to:

  • Remove any content that violates these child safety standards without notice.
  • Permanently suspend accounts found to have engaged in CSAE-related activity.
  • Disclose information to NCMEC and law enforcement as required by applicable law, including under 18 U.S.C. § 2258A.

7. Contact

For questions or concerns about child safety on Park Pair, contact us at privacy@parkpair.app.
